Contacts

There are many ways to contact us. If you want to contribute the project or want to tell us something important, you are free to use any. Note, that none of the team members provides private support.

Recommended ways:

  • Write a message / create a topic in an appropriate forum
  • Sent us an email to m {dog *} umineko-project {dot} org
  • Communicate with us through YouTube or Google Plus (links on the top)

Other possibilities:

  • Use XMPP (Jabber) talk.umineko-project.org
  • Try mailing team member privately (nickname {dog} umineko-project {dot} org)

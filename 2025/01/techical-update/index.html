Technical Update

UPDATED 2025-01-04 vit9696

Dear users,

We use this opportunity to wish you happy New Year holidays and to express our gratitude to the community contributions over the last four years.

While our project is over and there are no plans for any further work, we still maintain the resources and provide minimal help to other teams translating the game into languages of their preference. As part of the Umineko Multilingua project in addition to Brazilian Portuguese and Chinese we are glad to see three more translations emerging:

  • Italian by Majotachi no Sub
  • Turkish by Witch Love
  • Spanish by Universo When They Cry

Other than that, our community members contributed several bugfixes to the game, and we updated the downloads page to include them all.

For security reasons we had to perform few website adjustments and archive the old community forums, which are now available in read-only mode. Further discussions will happen on GitHub.
